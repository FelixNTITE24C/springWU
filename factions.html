Felix' Warhammer List Building Guide

- Faction Unit Roles -

Leader: Captain (All variants) - Provides "Rites of Battle" for a 0CP Stratagem and hits hard in melee. Objective Holder: Intercessors - Their "Sticky Objectives" ability allows them to capture a point and move away while still holding it. Trade Unit: Assault Intercessors with Jump Packs - Cheap, fast, and high volume of attacks. They jump in, disrupt a unit, and if they die, you’ve only lost minimal points. Anti-Tank: Gladiator Lancer - The gold standard. Its Lancer Laser Destroyer is S12, AP-4, and highly reliable with its built-in rerolls. Secondary Scorer: Scout Squad - "Guerrilla Tactics" allows them to go into Strategic Reserves at the end of the turn and redeploy where the mission needs them. Punchback: Bladeguard Veteran Squad - High-quality melee attacks and an invulnerable save. They sit behind a wall and wait for the enemy to touch the center objective. Anvil: Terminator Squad - With a 2+ Save, 4+ Invulnerable Save, and 3 Wounds each, they are the classic "brick" that takes forever to kill. Transport: Impulsor - A fast, shielded hover-tank that gets your specialized infantry (like Hellblasters) into position safely.

Leader High Marshal Helbrecht The ultimate Leader. He adds +1 Strength to his unit’s melee weapons and deals massive Critical Hits on 5s. He turns a good unit into a blender. Objective Holder Primaris Crusader Squad With up to 20 models in a single unit, their sheer OC (Objective Control) footprint is massive. They don't just hold points; they swamp them. Trade Unit Sword Brethren (Small Squad) While expensive, they function as an elite trade unit. Using their "Vow-Seekers" ability, they can punch up and delete units twice their cost before being targeted. Anti-Tank Black Templars Repulsor Executioner While the Lancer is great, the Templar version of the Executioner benefits from the Uphold the Honour of the Emperor vow, giving this heavy tank a 6+ Feel No Pain. Punchback The Emperor’s Champion The premier "Precision" counter-attacker. If an enemy character leads a charge into your lines, he steps out and challenges them to a duel they usually lose. Anvil Primaris Crusader Squad (with Grimaldus) When joined by Cenobyte Servitors, this unit gets a 5+ Feel No Pain. A 20-man brick with a 5+ FNP is one of the hardest "Anvils" to shift in the entire game. Transport Black Templar Impulsor Specifically valued here because it can carry Multi-Meltas (a Templar-only upgrade), turning a simple transport into a mobile anti-tank threat.

Leader Commander Dante The ultimate force multiplier. He grants +1 to hit and +1 to Battle-shock tests for his unit, and his "Lord Regent" ability makes him a nightmare in combat. Trade Unit Death Company Marines (with Jump Packs) The quintessential trade unit. Armed with Power Fists or Inferno Pistols, they strike with terrifying efficiency. You launch them at a target, they delete it, and they die in glory. Anti-Tank Librarian Dreadnought A unique "psychic" anti-tank solution. While its halberd is strong, its real value is the Wings of Sanguinius power, which can teleport an infantry unit across the board to deliver a killing blow. Secondary Scorer Sanguinary Guard While elite, their high mobility and ability to grant -1 to hit and wound (if a Warlord is nearby) makes them surprisingly good at landing in a backfield and surviving just long enough to score. Punchback Assault Intercessors (Death Company) On foot, these guys are the ultimate "don't touch my objective" unit. Their Red Thirst (+1 Strength/Attacks on the charge) makes them flatten anything that gets close. Anvil Death Company Dreadnought Built-in Feel No Pain 6+ and the ability to fight back immediately after being hit (the Frenzied Reprisal rule) makes it a stubborn, angry roadblock. Transport Baal Predator While technically a tank, it serves the role of a "Clearance Transport." It leads the way for your actual transports, using its Overcharged Engines and flamers to wipe out enemy screens so your melee units can charge.

Leader Azrael One of the best Leaders in the game. He generates 1 extra CP every command phase and gives his unit a 4+ Invulnerable Save and Sustained Hits 1. Objective Holder Inner Circle Companions While they are melee focused, their "Inner Circle" ability makes them harder to hit. They act as a "contested objective" holder that enemies struggle to shoot off a point. Trade Unit Ravenwing Black Knights Mounted on bikes and armed with Anti-Monster/Vehicle 4+ triggers on their Corvus Hammers, they can zoom 12", delete a target, and occupy the enemy's attention. Anti-Tank Ravenwing Darkshroud While not a damage dealer itself, it is a "Support Anti-Tank" tool. Its aura of Stealth and Cover protects your actual Anti-Tank (like Repulsors) from being picked off. Secondary Scorer Ravenwing Land Speeder Vengeance Fast and versatile. Its high movement allows it to zip into corners for Deploy Teleport Homers, while its Plasma Storm Battery can still harass the enemy. Punchback Deathwing Knights The ultimate deterrent. If an enemy takes an objective, these guys charge in with maces and shields. They hit like a truck and are incredibly difficult to kill in return. Anvil Deathwing Terminator Squad Unlike standard Terminators, these can ignore hit modifiers and have the Watcher in the Dark for a one-time 4+ Feel No Pain against Mortal Wounds. Transport Stormraven Gunship While standard, it is iconic for Dark Angels to ferry a Dreadnought and a squad of Deathwing Knights directly into the heart of the enemy.

Leader Kaldor Draigo The king of the "surgical strike." He allows his unit to add 3" to their Charge roll when arriving from Deep Strike, making a successful charge almost guaranteed. Objective Holder Strike Squad Their "Sanctifying Ritual" grants Sticky Objectives. They can capture a point and teleport away to fight elsewhere, leaving the objective under your control. Trade Unit Interceptor Squad With a 12" move and the ability to move again after shooting, they are high-speed harassers. They zip in to wipe a screen or light unit and then reposition. Anti-Tank Nemesis Dreadknight Grey Knights lack high-S guns, so the Dreadknight is your heavy hitter. With the Heavy Psycannon and Nemesis Greatsword, it’s your best bet for downing armor. Secondary Scorer Brotherhood Techmarine A cheap individual character who can hide in the back to "Investigate Signals" or repair a Dreadknight, freeing up your expensive squads to go killing. Punchback Brotherhood Librarians Their Vortex of Doom psychic power deals massive Mortal Wounds. They don't even need to charge; they just teleport nearby and "nuke" whatever touched your lines. Anvil Brotherhood Terminators Every model has 3 Wounds and a 4+ Invulnerable save. Most importantly, they bring back one dead model every Command Phase, making them a nightmare to fully wipe out. Transport Grey Knights Land Raider Essential for the "Assault Ramp" rule. It allows your Paladins or Terminators to disembark and charge in the same turn, bypassing the need for a risky 9" Deep Strike charge.

Leader Morvenn Vahl A supreme force multiplier. She grants full re-rolls to Hit and Wound for her unit of Paragon Suits, turning them into the most lethal unit in your army. Objective Holder Battle Sisters Squad The gold standard. They generate Miracle Dice just for standing on objectives and have OC 2, making them difficult to out-score. Trade Unit Repentia Squad Glass cannons in their purest form. They have no armor but hit with S6, AP-2, D2 eviscerators. You throw them at an elite unit, they kill it, and they die for the Emperor. Anvil Sacresants / Crusaders While Sisters aren't naturally tough, Sacresants have 2+ armor and a 4+ Invulnerable save. Crusaders are incredibly cheap "chaff anvils" used to soak up a single heavy charge. Transport Sororitas Rhino Vital for the army. Because Sisters are fragile (T3), a Rhino is mandatory to get Repentia or Dominions across the board without being shot off the table.

Leader Shield-Captain The tactical heart. His "Strategic Mastery" allows you to use a Stratagem for 0CP even if it’s already been used, which is vital for an army that relies on defensive tricks. Objective Holder Custodian Guard These are the best "Objective Holders" in the game. They can re-roll wound rolls while on an objective and can use their Sentinel Blade and shield for a 4+ Invulnerable save. Trade Unit Venatari Custodes While expensive for a "trade," their ability to drop from the sky and move for free after shooting makes them perfect for jumping in, deleting a backline unit, and forcing the enemy to over-commit to kill them. Anti-Tank Caladius Grav-Tank Since Custodes are primarily melee, the Caladius with the Twin Iliastus Accelerator Cannon provides the long-range S12 punch needed to crack heavy armor before you charge. Secondary Scorer Prosecutors The only "cheap" unit you have. These Sisters of Silence are your primary action-performers, freeing up your 200pt+ Custodian squads to actually fight. Punchback Allarus Custodians The "Golden Terminators." Their Slayers of Tyrants ability lets them re-roll hits/wounds against Characters/Monsters. They can also vanish and "re-deploy" to respond to any threat. Anvil Wardens The ultimate roadblock. Once per game, they have a 4+ Feel No Pain. Combined with their innate -1 to Wound from high-strength attacks, they simply refuse to die. Transport Venerable Land Raider Essential for getting slow infantry safely into the mid-board. Its Godhammer Lascannons also help out with the Anti-Tank role, making it a very efficient point investment.

Leader Tech-Priest Manipulus The ultimate offensive buffer. He provides his unit with Lethal Hits and a once-per-game 4+ Invulnerable save, turning a standard squad into a tank-shredding threat. Objective Holder Skitarii Vanguard Their "Rad-Saturation" aura reduces the Objective Control (OC) and Toughness of nearby enemies, making them elite at "stealing" points even if they don't kill the enemy. Trade Unit Sydonian Raiders (Dragoons) Fast, annoying, and surprisingly tanky with Stealth. They can charge into enemy lines to tie up shooters, fully expecting to explode and cause chaos. Anti-Tank Ironstrider Ballistarii The premier long-range sniper. Equipped with Cognis Lascannons, they are highly mobile, cheap platforms that provide the high-Strength shots the army needs. Secondary Scorer Serberys Sulphurhounds These mechanical hounds have a huge movement range and the Scouts 6" ability. They are perfect for zipping into corners or performing "Tactical Missions." Punchback Sicarian Infiltrators While AdMech isn't a "melee army," these hunters can deploy anywhere. Use them to jump out of the shadows and charge whatever is threatening your gunline. Anvil Kastelan Robots High Toughness and multiple wounds. When paired with a Cybernetica Datasmith, they can swap into a defensive protocol that makes them nearly impossible to move. Transport Skorpius Dunerider A vital "assault boat." It has a massive volume of anti-infantry fire (stubbers) and gives the unit inside a re-roll to wound when they disembark.

Leader Lord Solar Leontus The ultimate Leader. He is the only model who can give orders to Vehicles, Super-heavies, and Infantry alike. He also generates an extra CP and allows for redeploys. Objective Holder Cadian Shock Troops Their "Shock Troops" ability gives them Sticky Objectives. In a Guard army, you want to tag an objective and then move your bodies forward to die for it elsewhere. Trade Unit Catachan Jungle Fighters Dirt cheap and surprisingly fast with their Scouts 6" move. You send them into the mid-board to move-block the enemy and die, buying time for your tanks to shoot. Anti-Tank Leman Russ Vanquisher The "big game hunter." Its cannon is S18 with massive damage. Alternatively, the Ursula Creed + Fields of Fire stratagem combo lets even your basic guns punch up. Secondary Scorer Gaunt’s Ghosts A unique "Lone Operative" unit. They can vanish from the board and reappear in a different corner every turn, making them the best secondary scorers in the game. Punchback Bullgryns While the Guard is a shooting army, Bullgryns are your "No" button. With a 4+ invulnerable save and -1 damage taken, they bash anything that touches your lines. Anvil Rogal Dorn Battle Tank A massive chunk of steel. Its "Ablative Armour" ability lets it change the Damage characteristic of a failed save to 0 once per game, making it incredibly frustrating to kill. Transport Chimera The classic APC. It’s tough enough to act as a mobile wall, and its "Mobile Command Post" rule lets an Officer inside still issue orders to the troops on the ground.

Leader Inquisitor Coteaz A premier utility Leader. He allows you to "tax" your opponent; whenever they gain a CP through an ability, he can potentially steal it for you. Objective Holder Inquisitorial Agents Cheap and efficient. When led by an Inquisitor, they become much more durable. They are best used to camp home objectives or provide a screen for your heavy hitters. Trade Unit Eversor Assassin The ultimate "fire and forget" missile. He has a massive threat range and can explode when he dies. You send him in to butcher a squad and disrupt the enemy's tempo. Anti-Tank Vindicare Assassin While he won't one-shot a Land Raider, his "Shieldbreaker" round ignores invulnerable saves. He is "Anti-Tank" in the sense that he removes the Leaders that make enemy tanks efficient. Secondary Scorer Callidus Assassin Perhaps the best utility piece in the game. Her "Polymorphine" ability allows her to go into reserves at the end of the enemy turn and reappear anywhere to score points. Punchback Culexus Assassin A specialized counter-attacker. His "Soulless" aura makes him nearly impossible to hit and he shuts down Psykers, making him a perfect "No" button for center-board fights. Anvil Subductor Squad These are Arbites with massive shields. They have a 4+ Invulnerable save and are designed specifically to stand in a doorway or on a point and refuse to move. Transport Inquisitorial Chimera Since most Agents are T3 human-equivalent, the Chimera is mandatory for protection. It allows your Inquisitors to move up-board without being sniped.

Leader Canis Rex The ultimate solo Leader. He doesn't need a squad; his "Chainbreaker" ability allows him to use a Stratagem for 0CP every turn, and he hits on a 2+ regardless of modifiers. Objective Holder Armiger Helverin While big knights have high OC, Helverins are your "backfield" holders. Their autocannons have massive range, letting them sit on a home objective and still contribute to the fight. Trade Unit Armiger Warglaive The closest thing Knights have to a trade unit. They are fast (12") and relatively "cheap." You send them in to melt a tank with their Thermal Spear and tie up the enemy in melee. Anti-Tank Knight Valiant The "Dominus" class solution. Its Conflagration Cannon and Thundercoil Harpoon are designed to delete the largest targets in the game at close range. Secondary Scorer Callidus Assassin (Allied) Since every Knight is expensive, you almost always bring an Allied Agent. The Callidus teleports around to score points so your 400pt Knights don't have to waste time doing "actions." Punchback Knight Gallant A pure melee beast. If an enemy dares to charge your lines, the Gallant uses its WS 2+ and massive Strike profile to flatten them before they can swing twice. Anvil Knight Castellan With T12, 24 Wounds, and a 2+ Armor Save, this is the hardest unit to shift. It sits in your deployment zone and acts as a fire-base that the enemy simply cannot ignore or easily destroy. Transport N/A (The "Wall" Role) Knights don't use Transports. Instead, your big Knights act as "Mobile Cover" or "Bodyguards" for your Armigers using the Bondsman abilities.

Leader Be'lakor The ultimate lynchpin. His "Shadow of Chaos" aura protects your units from being shot unless the enemy is within 18", and he acts as a mobile teleport beacon for your other units. Objective Holder Nurglings The best "nuisance" unit in the game. While they have OC 0, their Infiltrate and "Cloud of Flies" (-1 to hit) make them perfect for move-blocking and sitting on points to deny enemy scores. Trade Unit Fiends of Slaanesh Blistering speed and high damage. They can hit a flank, tie up a unit with their "Soporific Musk" (preventing Fall Back), and deal enough damage to earn their points back before they vanish. Anti-Tank Skarbrand A literal wrecking ball. His axes can reach Strength 16, and he prevents enemies from Falling Back, ensuring he can chew through the toughest hulls in a single combat phase. Secondary Scorer The Changeling A defensive genius. He has Lone Operative, can force enemies to fail their shooting, and is cheap enough to sit in a corner scoring Tactical Missions all game. Punchback Bloodletters The classic "glass cannon." They are fragile, but if you drop them from Deep Strike near a contested point, their S5 AP-2 D2 attacks will blend almost any infantry unit. Anvil Great Unclean One With T12, 20+ Wounds, and a 4+ Feel No Pain (if equipped with the Everstave enhancement), he is the toughest "brick" in the game. He simply sits in the center and lives. Transport Shalaxi Helbane Daemons don't use vehicles; their "Transport" is Deep Strike. Shalaxi acts as a high-speed delivery system for death, using a massive move characteristic to close gaps instantly.
