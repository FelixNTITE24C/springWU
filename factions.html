Home Unit Roles Example Armies

- Space Marines -

Space Marines

Leader: Captain (All variants)
Provides "Rites of Battle" for a 0CP Stratagem and hits hard in melee.

Objective Holder: Intercessors
Their "Sticky Objectives" ability allows them to capture a point and move away while still holding it.

Trade Unit: Assault Intercessors with Jump Packs
Cheap, fast, and high volume of attacks. They jump in, disrupt a unit, and if they die, you’ve only lost minimal points.

Anti-Tank: Gladiator Lancer
The gold standard. Its Lancer Laser Destroyer is S12, AP-4, and highly reliable with its built-in rerolls.

Secondary Scorer: Scout Squad
"Guerrilla Tactics" allows them to go into Strategic Reserves at the end of the turn and redeploy where the mission needs them.

Punchback: Bladeguard Veteran Squad
High-quality melee attacks and an invulnerable save. They sit behind a wall and wait for the enemy to touch the center objective.

Anvil: Terminator Squad
With a 2+ Save, 4+ Invulnerable Save, and 3 Wounds each, they are the classic "brick" that takes forever to kill.

Transport: Impulsor
A fast, shielded hover-tank that gets your specialized infantry (like Hellblasters) into position safely.

Black Templars

Leader: High Marshal Helbrecht
The ultimate Leader. He adds +1 Strength to his unit’s melee weapons and deals massive Critical Hits on 5s. He turns a good unit into a blender.

Objective Holder: Primaris Crusader Squad
With up to 20 models in a single unit, their sheer OC (Objective Control) footprint is massive. They don't just hold points; they swamp them.

Trade Unit: Sword Brethren (Small Squad)
While expensive, they function as an elite trade unit. Using their "Vow-Seekers" ability, they can punch up and delete units twice their cost before being targeted.

Anti-Tank: Black Templars Repulsor Executioner
While the Lancer is great, the Templar version of the Executioner benefits from the Uphold the Honour of the Emperor vow, giving this heavy tank a 6+ Feel No Pain.

Punchback: The Emperor’s Champion
The premier "Precision" counter-attacker. If an enemy character leads a charge into your lines, he steps out and challenges them to a duel they usually lose.

Anvil: Primaris Crusader Squad (with Grimaldus)
When joined by Cenobyte Servitors, this unit gets a 5+ Feel No Pain. A 20-man brick with a 5+ FNP is one of the hardest "Anvils" to shift in the entire game.

Transport: Black Templar Impulsor
Specifically valued here because it can carry Multi-Meltas (a Templar-only upgrade), turning a simple transport into a mobile anti-tank threat.

Blood Angels

Leader: Commander Dante
The ultimate force multiplier. He grants +1 to hit and +1 to Battle-shock tests for his unit, and his "Lord Regent" ability makes him a nightmare in combat.

Trade Unit: Death Company Marines (with Jump Packs)
The quintessential trade unit. Armed with Power Fists or Inferno Pistols, they strike with terrifying efficiency. You launch them at a target, they delete it, and they die in glory.

Anti-Tank: Librarian Dreadnought
A unique "psychic" anti-tank solution. While its halberd is strong, its real value is the Wings of Sanguinius power, which can teleport an infantry unit across the board to deliver a killing blow.

Secondary Scorer: Sanguinary Guard
While elite, their high mobility and ability to grant -1 to hit and wound (if a Warlord is nearby) makes them surprisingly good at landing in a backfield and surviving just long enough to score.

Punchback: Assault Intercessors (Death Company)
On foot, these guys are the ultimate "don't touch my objective" unit. Their Red Thirst (+1 Strength/Attacks on the charge) makes them flatten anything that gets close.

Anvil: Death Company Dreadnought
Built-in Feel No Pain 6+ and the ability to fight back immediately after being hit (the Frenzied Reprisal rule) makes it a stubborn, angry roadblock.

Transport: Baal Predator
While technically a tank, it serves the role of a "Clearance Transport." It leads the way for your actual transports, using its Overcharged Engines and flamers to wipe out enemy screens so your melee units can charge.

Dark Angels

Leader: Azrael
One of the best Leaders in the game. He generates 1 extra CP every command phase and gives his unit a 4+ Invulnerable Save and Sustained Hits 1.

Objective Holder: Inner Circle Companions
While they are melee focused, their "Inner Circle" ability makes them harder to hit. They act as a "contested objective" holder that enemies struggle to shoot off a point.

Trade Unit: Ravenwing Black Knights
Mounted on bikes and armed with Anti-Monster/Vehicle 4+ triggers on their Corvus Hammers, they can zoom 12", delete a target, and occupy the enemy's attention.

Anti-Tank: Ravenwing Darkshroud
While not a damage dealer itself, it is a "Support Anti-Tank" tool. Its aura of Stealth and Cover protects your actual Anti-Tank (like Repulsors) from being picked off.

Secondary Scorer: Ravenwing Land Speeder Vengeance
Fast and versatile. Its high movement allows it to zip into corners for Deploy Teleport Homers, while its Plasma Storm Battery can still harass the enemy.

Punchback: Deathwing Knights
The ultimate deterrent. If an enemy takes an objective, these guys charge in with maces and shields. They hit like a truck and are incredibly difficult to kill in return.

Anvil: Deathwing Terminator Squad
Unlike standard Terminators, these can ignore hit modifiers and have the Watcher in the Dark for a one-time 4+ Feel No Pain against Mortal Wounds.

Transport: Stormraven Gunship
While standard, it is iconic for Dark Angels to ferry a Dreadnought and a squad of Deathwing Knights directly into the heart of the enemy.

Grey Knight

Leader: Kaldor Draigo
The king of the "surgical strike." He allows his unit to add 3" to their Charge roll when arriving from Deep Strike, making a successful charge almost guaranteed.

Objective Holder: Strike Squad
Their "Sanctifying Ritual" grants Sticky Objectives. They can capture a point and teleport away to fight elsewhere, leaving the objective under your control.

Trade Unit: Interceptor Squad
With a 12" move and the ability to move again after shooting, they are high-speed harassers. They zip in to wipe a screen or light unit and then reposition.

Anti-Tank: Nemesis Dreadknight
Grey Knights lack high-S guns, so the Dreadknight is your heavy hitter. With the Heavy Psycannon and Nemesis Greatsword, it’s your best bet for downing armor.

Secondary Scorer: Brotherhood Techmarine
A cheap individual character who can hide in the back to "Investigate Signals" or repair a Dreadknight, freeing up your expensive squads to go killing.

Punchback: Brotherhood Librarians
Their Vortex of Doom psychic power deals massive Mortal Wounds. They don't even need to charge; they just teleport nearby and "nuke" whatever touched your lines.

Anvil: Brotherhood Terminators
Every model has 3 Wounds and a 4+ Invulnerable save. Most importantly, they bring back one dead model every Command Phase, making them a nightmare to fully wipe out.

Transport: Grey Knights Land Raider
Essential for the "Assault Ramp" rule. It allows your Paladins or Terminators to disembark and charge in the same turn, bypassing the need for a risky 9" Deep Strike charge.

Space Wolves

Leader: Logan Grimnar on Stormrider
A massive centerpiece leader. Once per game, he grants his entire army a charge and melee hit reroll, turning the whole "pack" into an unstoppable force.

Objective Holder: Grey Hunters
Unlike standard Intercessors, they can take two special weapons (like Meltaguns) and still have chainswords. They hold points while remaining a credible threat to anything that tries to land near them.

Trade Unit: Fenrisian Wolves
At only 30 points for 5 models, these are the ultimate trade units. They are fast "screens" used to block enemy movement or die in place of your expensive heroes.

Anti-Tank: Wulfen Dreadnought
Equipped with a Blizzard Shield and Great Fenrisian Axe, this is a "suicide" anti-tank piece. It runs straight at a Knight or Land Raider and chops it in half.

Secondary Scorer: Hounds of Morkai
Specialist anti-psyker scouts. They are fast, have Infiltrate, and can use "Morkai’s Howl" to force Battle-shock tests, making them great at stealing points and scoring tactical missions.

Punchback: Wulfen
These are your "Counter-Charge" kings. They move fast and, most importantly, they Fight on Death. If an enemy charges and kills them, the Wulfen still swing back before being removed.

Anvil: Thunderwolf Cavalry
With 4 Wounds each, a 4+ Invulnerable Save (Storm Shields), and the ability to add a Wolf Lord for extra durability, they are the most mobile "Anvils" in the game.

Transport: Stormfang Gunship
A dedicated flyer that acts as a mobile fortress. It carries your specialized infantry while providing massive strafing fire with its Helfrost Destructor.

- Imperium -

Adepta Sororitas

Leader: Morvenn Vahl
A supreme force multiplier. She grants full re-rolls to Hit and Wound for her unit of Paragon Suits, turning them into the most lethal unit in your army.

Objective Holder: Battle Sisters Squad
The gold standard. They generate Miracle Dice just for standing on objectives and have OC 2, making them difficult to out-score.

Trade Unit: Repentia Squad
Glass cannons in their purest form. They have no armor but hit with S6, AP-2, D2 eviscerators. You throw them at an elite unit, they kill it, and they die for the Emperor.

Anti-Tank: Castigator
Specifically with the Battle Cannon. It provides high-strength, long-range fire that can pick off light tanks, while Retributors with Multi-Meltas provide the close-range punch.

Secondary Scorer: Seraphim Squad
Armed with jump packs, they can use their "Skystrike" ability to shoot and then move another 6", allowing them to score Behind Enemy Lines and hide back in cover.

Punchback: Arco-Flagellants
An absolute blender of attacks. If an enemy charges your front line, these crazed zealots charge in with a massive volume of attacks to wipe the unit out.

Anvil: Sacresants / Crusaders
While Sisters aren't naturally tough, Sacresants have 2+ armor and a 4+ Invulnerable save. Crusaders are incredibly cheap "chaff anvils" used to soak up a single heavy charge.

Transport: Sororitas Rhino
Vital for the army. Because Sisters are fragile (T3), a Rhino is mandatory to get Repentia or Dominions across the board without being shot off the table.

Adeptus Custodes

Leader: Shield-Captain
The tactical heart. His "Strategic Mastery" allows you to use a Stratagem for 0CP even if it’s already been used, which is vital for an army that relies on defensive tricks.

Objective Holder: Custodian Guard
These are the best "Objective Holders" in the game. They can re-roll wound rolls while on an objective and can use their Sentinel Blade and shield for a 4+ Invulnerable save.

Trade Unit: Venatari Custodes
While expensive for a "trade," their ability to drop from the sky and move for free after shooting makes them perfect for jumping in, deleting a backline unit, and forcing the enemy to over-commit to kill them.

Anti-Tank: Caladius Grav-Tank
Since Custodes are primarily melee, the Caladius with the Twin Iliastus Accelerator Cannon provides the long-range S12 punch needed to crack heavy armor before you charge.

Secondary Scorer: Prosecutors
The only "cheap" unit you have. These Sisters of Silence are your primary action-performers, freeing up your 200pt+ Custodian squads to actually fight.

Punchback: Allarus Custodians
The "Golden Terminators." Their Slayers of Tyrants ability lets them re-roll hits/wounds against Characters/Monsters. They can also vanish and "re-deploy" to respond to any threat.

Anvil: Wardens
The ultimate roadblock. Once per game, they have a 4+ Feel No Pain. Combined with their innate -1 to Wound from high-strength attacks, they simply refuse to die.

Transport: Venerable Land Raider
Essential for getting slow infantry safely into the mid-board. Its Godhammer Lascannons also help out with the Anti-Tank role, making it a very efficient point investment.

Adeptus Mechanicus

Leader: Tech-Priest Manipulus
The ultimate offensive buffer. He provides his unit with Lethal Hits and a once-per-game 4+ Invulnerable save, turning a standard squad into a tank-shredding threat.

Objective Holder: Skitarii Vanguard
Their "Rad-Saturation" aura reduces the Objective Control (OC) and Toughness of nearby enemies, making them elite at "stealing" points even if they don't kill the enemy.

Trade Unit: Sydonian Raiders (Dragoons)
Fast, annoying, and surprisingly tanky with Stealth. They can charge into enemy lines to tie up shooters, fully expecting to explode and cause chaos.

Anti-Tank: Ironstrider Ballistarii
The premier long-range sniper. Equipped with Cognis Lascannons, they are highly mobile, cheap platforms that provide the high-Strength shots the army needs.

Secondary Scorer: Serberys Sulphurhounds
These mechanical hounds have a huge movement range and the Scouts 6" ability. They are perfect for zipping into corners or performing "Tactical Missions."

Punchback: Sicarian Infiltrators
While AdMech isn't a "melee army," these hunters can deploy anywhere. Use them to jump out of the shadows and charge whatever is threatening your gunline.

Anvil: Kastelan Robots
High Toughness and multiple wounds. When paired with a Cybernetica Datasmith, they can swap into a defensive protocol that makes them nearly impossible to move.

Transport: Skorpius Dunerider
A vital "assault boat." It has a massive volume of anti-infantry fire (stubbers) and gives the unit inside a re-roll to wound when they disembark.

Astra Militarum

Leader: Lord Solar Leontus
The ultimate Leader. He is the only model who can give orders to Vehicles, Super-heavies, and Infantry alike. He also generates an extra CP and allows for redeploys.

Objective Holder: Cadian Shock Troops
Their "Shock Troops" ability gives them Sticky Objectives. In a Guard army, you want to tag an objective and then move your bodies forward to die for it elsewhere.

Trade Unit: Catachan Jungle Fighters
Dirt cheap and surprisingly fast with their Scouts 6" move. You send them into the mid-board to move-block the enemy and die, buying time for your tanks to shoot.

Anti-Tank: Leman Russ Vanquisher
The "big game hunter." Its cannon is S18 with massive damage. Alternatively, the Ursula Creed + Fields of Fire stratagem combo lets even your basic guns punch up.

Secondary Scorer: Gaunt’s Ghosts
A unique "Lone Operative" unit. They can vanish from the board and reappear in a different corner every turn, making them the best secondary scorers in the game.

Punchback: Bullgryns
While the Guard is a shooting army, Bullgryns are your "No" button. With a 4+ invulnerable save and -1 damage taken, they bash anything that touches your lines.

Anvil: Rogal Dorn Battle Tank
A massive chunk of steel. Its "Ablative Armour" ability lets it change the Damage characteristic of a failed save to 0 once per game, making it incredibly frustrating to kill.

Transport: Chimera
The classic APC. It’s tough enough to act as a mobile wall, and its "Mobile Command Post" rule lets an Officer inside still issue orders to the troops on the ground.

Imperial Agents

Leader: Inquisitor Coteaz
A premier utility Leader. He allows you to "tax" your opponent; whenever they gain a CP through an ability, he can potentially steal it for you.

Objective Holder: Inquisitorial Agents
Cheap and efficient. When led by an Inquisitor, they become much more durable. They are best used to camp home objectives or provide a screen for your heavy hitters.

Trade Unit: Eversor Assassin
The ultimate "fire and forget" missile. He has a massive threat range and can explode when he dies. You send him in to butcher a squad and disrupt the enemy's tempo.

Anti-Tank: Vindicare Assassin
While he won't one-shot a Land Raider, his "Shieldbreaker" round ignores invulnerable saves. He is "Anti-Tank" in the sense that he removes the Leaders that make enemy tanks efficient.

Secondary Scorer: Callidus Assassin
Perhaps the best utility piece in the game. Her "Polymorphine" ability allows her to go into reserves at the end of the enemy turn and reappear anywhere to score points.

Punchback: Culexus Assassin
A specialized counter-attacker. His "Soulless" aura makes him nearly impossible to hit and he shuts down Psykers, making him a perfect "No" button for center-board fights.

Anvil: Subductor Squad
These are Arbites with massive shields. They have a 4+ Invulnerable save and are designed specifically to stand in a doorway or on a point and refuse to move.

Transport: Inquisitorial Chimera
Since most Agents are T3 human-equivalent, the Chimera is mandatory for protection. It allows your Inquisitors to move up-board without being sniped.

Imperial Knights

Leader: Canis Rex
The ultimate solo Leader. He doesn't need a squad; his "Chainbreaker" ability allows him to use a Stratagem for 0CP every turn, and he hits on a 2+ regardless of modifiers.

Objective Holder: Armiger Helverin
While big knights have high OC, Helverins are your "backfield" holders. Their autocannons have massive range, letting them sit on a home objective and still contribute to the fight.

Trade Unit: Armiger Warglaive
The closest thing Knights have to a trade unit. They are fast (12") and relatively "cheap." You send them in to melt a tank with their Thermal Spear and tie up the enemy in melee.

Anti-Tank: Knight Valiant
The "Dominus" class solution. Its Conflagration Cannon and Thundercoil Harpoon are designed to delete the largest targets in the game at close range.

Secondary Scorer: Callidus Assassin (Allied)
Since every Knight is expensive, you almost always bring an Allied Agent. The Callidus teleports around to score points so your 400pt Knights don't have to waste time doing "actions."

Punchback: Knight Gallant
A pure melee beast. If an enemy dares to charge your lines, the Gallant uses its WS 2+ and massive Strike profile to flatten them before they can swing twice.

Anvil: Knight Castellan
With T12, 24 Wounds, and a 2+ Armor Save, this is the hardest unit to shift. It sits in your deployment zone and acts as a fire-base that the enemy simply cannot ignore or easily destroy.

Transport: N/A (The "Wall" Role)
Knights don't use Transports. Instead, your big Knights act as "Mobile Cover" or "Bodyguards" for your Armigers using the Bondsman abilities.

- Chaos -

Chaos Daemons

Leader: Be'lakor
The ultimate lynchpin. His "Shadow of Chaos" aura protects your units from being shot unless the enemy is within 18", and he acts as a mobile teleport beacon for your other units.

Objective Holder: Nurglings
The best "nuisance" unit in the game. While they have OC 0, their Infiltrate and "Cloud of Flies" (-1 to hit) make them perfect for move-blocking and sitting on points to deny enemy scores.

Trade Unit: Fiends of Slaanesh
Blistering speed and high damage. They can hit a flank, tie up a unit with their "Soporific Musk" (preventing Fall Back), and deal enough damage to earn their points back before they vanish.

Anti-Tank: Skarbrand
A literal wrecking ball. His axes can reach Strength 16, and he prevents enemies from Falling Back, ensuring he can chew through the toughest hulls in a single combat phase.

Secondary Scorer: The Changeling
A defensive genius. He has Lone Operative, can force enemies to fail their shooting, and is cheap enough to sit in a corner scoring Tactical Missions all game.

Punchback: Bloodletters
The classic "glass cannon." They are fragile, but if you drop them from Deep Strike near a contested point, their S5 AP-2 D2 attacks will blend almost any infantry unit.

Anvil: Great Unclean One
With T12, 20+ Wounds, and a 4+ Feel No Pain (if equipped with the Everstave enhancement), he is the toughest "brick" in the game. He simply sits in the center and lives.

Transport: Shalaxi Helbane
Daemons don't use vehicles; their "Transport" is Deep Strike. Shalaxi acts as a high-speed delivery system for death, using a massive move characteristic to close gaps instantly.

Chaos Space Marines

Leader: Abaddon the Despoiler
The ultimate aura bot. He grants a 4+ Invulnerable save, rerolls to hits, or Leadership buffs. He makes any unit he joins the center of the board's gravity.

Objective Holder: Cultist Mob
Dirt cheap and expendable. Their "Sticky Objectives" ability lets them tag a point and run away, or just sit there as a "human shield" for your more expensive units.

Trade Unit: Chosen
The "Swiss Army Knife" of CSM. They can Advance, Shoot, and Charge in the same turn. They hit incredibly hard and are fast enough to trade up into elite infantry.

Anti-Tank: Chaos Vindicator
Armed with the Demolisher Cannon, this tank is a S14 monster. With a Dark Pact for Sustained Hits, it can vaporize a Land Raider in a single volley.

Secondary Scorer: Warp Talons
Their "Warp Tether" ability allows them to go back into Strategic Reserves after killing a unit. They drop in, score a secondary, kill a screen, and vanish.

Punchback: Possessed
With Devastating Wounds and high movement, these are your premier counter-attackers. If an enemy touches your mid-board, the Possessed will move in and ignore their armor.

Anvil: Chaos Terminators
With a 2+ Save and 4+ Invulnerable, they are the classic brick. When paired with a Chaos Lord, they can use the Profane Zeal stratagem for free to survive even longer.

Transport: Chaos Rhino
Absolutely essential for getting Chosen or Legionaries into combat range without them being picked off by small arms fire.

Death Guard

Leader: Typhus
The ultimate value leader. He deals out mortal wounds with psychic bugs, buffs Poxwalkers, and is a melee powerhouse that is surprisingly difficult to kill.

Objective Holder: Plague Marines
With OC 2 and a wide array of specialized melee weapons and flamers, they don't just sit on points—they make it miserable for anyone to try and take them.

Trade Unit: Foetid Bloat-drone
Fast (for Death Guard), fly-capable, and equipped with Fleshmowers. It zips over terrain to shred an enemy unit, forcing your opponent to deal with it while your slow infantry plods forward.

Anti-Tank: Plagueburst Crawler
The iconic mortar tank. While its Mortar ignores line-of-sight to harass infantry, its Entropy Cannons are dedicated S12 tank-hunters that benefit from your Toughness-reduction auras.

Secondary Scorer: Nurglings (Allied)
Since Death Guard move like molasses, you need these "little lords." They infiltrate early to score points or move-block the enemy so your slow units can reach the mid-board.

Punchback: Deathshroud Terminators
The ultimate bodyguards. If an enemy charges your line, these guys hit back with scythes that have two profiles (one for hordes, one for armor). They are the premier "No" button.

Anvil: Blightlord Terminators
With a 2+ Save, 4+ Invulnerable, and the ability to reduce incoming wound rolls in certain detachments, they are a literal brick wall that stands in the center of the board.

Transport: Death Guard Rhino
Non-negotiable. Because Plague Marines move so slowly, the Rhino is the only way to ensure your "Trade" and "Objective" units reach the fight before the game ends.

Emperor's Children

Leader: Lucius the Eternal
The mandatory Leader. He grants his unit Fights First, making them an impossible target to charge. If he dies, he has a chance to deal Mortal Wounds back to his killer.

Objective Holder: Noise Marines
The iconic battleline. Their Blastmaster can shoot into combat, and their "Sonic Weapons" force Battle-shock tests, making it easier to hold points by stripping the enemy's OC.

Trade Unit: Chosen (Mark of Slaanesh)
With the ability to Advance, Shoot, and Charge, they are your primary scalpel. Using a Dark Pact for Sustained Hits on a 5+ (via the Slaanesh mark) ensures they delete their target.

Anti-Tank: Chaos Predator Destructor
Armed with Lascannons and the Predator Autocannon, it provides the heavy fire needed to soften up armor before your melee units arrive to finish the job.

Secondary Scorer: Warp Talons
The ultimate Slaaneshi hunters. Their speed and ability to go back into reserves make them perfect for "Behind Enemy Lines" or "Cleanse."

Punchback: Helbrute
When equipped with a multi-melta and fist, the Helbrute acts as a "buff totem." It grants nearby units both Lethal and Sustained hits, making your counter-charge units twice as lethal.

Anvil: Chaos Terminators
Best used with the Mark of Slaanesh. They aren't just tough; they can use the Unnatural Swiftness stratagem to fall back, shoot, and charge, ensuring they are never pinned down.

Transport: Chaos Rhino
Necessary for the "Sonic Delivery." Protecting your Noise Marines or Chosen during Turn 1 is the difference between a win and a loss for this glass-cannon army.

Thousand Sons

Leader: Ahriman
The ultimate utility Leader. He grants +1 to Wound for his unit and allows you to use one Cabal Ritual per game for 0 CaP, saving you 7–9 points for a game-changing play.

Objective Holder: Rubric Marines
The backbone of the army. They provide 1 Cabal Point each and have "Bringers of Change," which lets them re-roll Wound rolls of 1 (or full re-rolls if the enemy is on an objective).

Trade Unit: Tzaangor Enlightened
Fast, cheap, and expendable. Armed with Greatbows, they have Precision and move 12". They are perfect for sniping a low-health character and then dying to protect your expensive Wizards.

Anti-Tank: Mutalith Vortex Beast
This is your premier multi-tool. It provides a massive S10-S18 melee punch and acts as a "Cabal Beacon," doubling the range of your psychic rituals (like the one that strips armor).

Secondary Scorer: Chaos Spawn
Cheap, annoying, and they regenerate wounds. They are ideal for sitting in a corner to score Investigate Signals or Cleanse without wasting your precious Cabal-generating units.

Punchback: Infernal Master
While he looks like a Leader, his "Screamer Invocation" is a massive psychic flamer. If an enemy charges his unit, his Overwatch is one of the deadliest in the game.

Anvil: Scarab Occult Terminators
With a 2+ Save, 4+ Invulnerable, and the ability to reduce incoming damage via the Weaver of Fates ritual, they are a literal "psychic tank" that refuses to fall.

Transport: Thousand Sons Rhino
Essential for "Cabal Protection." It keeps your Rubric Marines and their Sorcerers safe on Turn 1 so you don't lose your Cabal Point generation early.

World Eaters

Leader: Lord Invocatus
The ultimate "Enabler." His Road of Eight Bloody Steps grants Scouts 6" to two infantry units, ensuring your slow Berzerkers hit the mid-board on Turn 1.

Objective Holder: Jakhals
Your "lowly" cultists. They have the Objective Secured equivalent and can "taint" objectives, allowing them to move off a point while still controlling it.

Trade Unit: Eightbound
High speed, high damage, low model count. They are designed to sprint 12"+, delete a unit of elite infantry, and force the enemy to over-allocate fire to stop them.

Anti-Tank: Exalted Eightbound
These are your scalpel. With S14 chainfists on the charge and an aura that prevents enemies from Falling Back, they ensure that once they touch a tank, it stays dead.

Secondary Scorer: Chaos Spawn
Dirt cheap and fast. They are perfect for sitting in a corner to score Investigate Signals so you don't have to waste your expensive killing machines on menial tasks.

Punchback: World Eaters Helbrute
His Frenzied Reprisal rule means every time he is targeted by an attack, he gets to shoot or fight back immediately. He is a nightmare to try and "chip" down.

Anvil: Angron
T11 with a 4+ Invulnerable and the ability to come back from the dead on a specific Blessings of Khorne roll. He is the ultimate "distraction" that refuses to stay down.

Transport: World Eaters Rhino
Mandatory. Despite your speed, Khorne Berzerkers are T4 with a 3+ save; they need the metal box to survive the "No Man's Land" crossing.

- Xenos -

Aeldari

Leader: Farseer
The ultimate manipulator. His "Branching Fates" ability allows you to turn any Fate Die roll into a 6, ensuring your Anti-Tank never misses and your saves always pass.

Objective Holder: Guardian Defenders
While fragile, they generate Fate Dice every turn they hold an objective. They are a "resource battery" that happens to hold a point.

Trade Unit: Howling Banshees
Blistering speed and Fights First. They can leap over terrain and delete an enemy infantry squad before they can swing back. If they die next turn, they've already done their job.

Anti-Tank: Fire Prism
The gold standard. Using "Linked Fire," one Prism can shoot for the whole squad, allowing you to hide the others behind walls while vaporizing a tank across the map.

Secondary Scorer: Swooping Hawks
The kings of utility. Their "Skyleap" ability lets them leave the board at the end of the turn and Deep Strike back in anywhere next turn to score points.

Punchback: Avatar of Khaine
A literal god of war. With T12 and a halved-damage rule, he sits in the mid-board. If the enemy touches your line, he counter-charges and flattens them.

Anvil: Wraithguard
One of the few "tough" units Aeldari have. With T7 and the "Warpspite" rule, they can shoot back at the unit that just shot them, making them very dangerous to engage.

Transport: Wave Serpent
One of the best transports in 40k. It’s fast, has a built-in Invulnerable save, and can "Twin-Link" its weapons to provide significant fire support.

Genestealer Cults

Leader: Primus
The tactical mastermind. He allows his unit to re-roll the Hit roll and provides a pre-game redeploy for three units, ensuring your "heist" starts perfectly.

Objective Holder: Neophyte Hybrids
The lifeblood of the cult. They have OC 2 and can return models to their squad every turn. They also regenerate CP when they hold objectives, fueling your stratagems.

Trade Unit: Acolyte Hybrids
These carry Heavy Mining Tools. They are cheap, high-damage "suicide" squads. They jump out of a sewer, delete a tank or elite squad, die, and then hopefully go into Cult Ambush to do it again.

Anti-Tank: Achilles Ridgerunners
While they have lasers and missiles, their real "Anti-Tank" power is their Spotter ability, which grants the rest of your army +1 Armor Penetration against a specific target.

Secondary Scorer: Purestrain Genestealers
With an 8" move and Infiltrate, they start the game exactly where they need to be to score points or move-block the enemy while your main force arrives.

Punchback: Aberrants
The rare GGC unit that isn't made of paper. With T6, 3 Wounds, and a 4+ Feel No Pain, they are the unit you send in to reclaim a point after the enemy has cleared your Neophytes.

Anvil: N/A (The "Respawn" Anvil)
GGC doesn't have true Anvils. Instead, your "Anvil" is the Cult Ambush mechanic. You "tank" damage by simply having more bodies that keep coming back from the dead.

Transport: Goliath Truck
Vital for protection. Its "Rugged Construction" gives it a 6+ Feel No Pain, and it allows your Acolytes to jump out and charge, keeping them safe from small-arms fire until the last second.

Leagues of Votann

Leader: Kâhl
The primary source of "Grim Efficiency." He applies Judgement Tokens to enemy units, allowing your entire army to hit more accurately and wound more easily.

Objective Holder: Hearthkyn Warriors
They are T5 (tougher than Marines) and have the Void Armour equivalent. Their "Luck has, Need keeps" ability allows them to hold points while generating extra efficiency.

Trade Unit: Hernkyn Pioneers
These hover-bikes are your primary "Scouts." They are fast (12") and can go into Strategic Reserves at the end of the enemy turn, allowing them to zip around and harass.

Anti-Tank: Hekaton Land Fortress
A massive rolling bunker. Its Heavy Magna-rail Cannon is one of the scariest guns in the game, capable of dealing massive damage that overflows into multiple models.

Secondary Scorer: Hernkyn Yaegirs
These are your infiltrators. They start the game up-board, allowing you to score early tactical missions while your slow main force moves into position.

Punchback: Cthonian Beserks
Pure melee aggression. They have no armor (only a 6+ save), but they have a 5+ Feel No Pain and Fight on Death. If the enemy charges your line, these guys clear the point.

Anvil: Einhyr Hearthguard
With T6, 2 Wounds, a 2+ Save, and a -1 to be wounded (if led by an Ancestor), they are arguably the most point-efficient "Anvil" in the game.

Transport: Sagitaur
A specialized light transport. It allows you to split your 10-man Warrior squads into two 5-man units, giving you more tactical flexibility and more units to "Trade."

Necrons

Leader: Overlord with Translocation Shroud
A tactical essential. He allows his unit to Auto-Advance 6" and pass through models/terrain, turning a slow infantry block into a high-speed threat.

Objective Holder: Necron Warriors
The ultimate tarpit. With a high model count and boosted reanimation, they are designed to sit on a point and simply "exist" longer than the enemy can shoot them.

Trade Unit: Skorpekh Destroyers
High-intensity melee specialists. They move fast and can use the "Whirling Blades" stratagem to stay alive long enough to delete an elite unit before they fall.

Anti-Tank: Lokhust Heavy Destroyers
Equipped with the Gauss Destructor, these are dedicated "one-shot" snipers. They are cheap, hover-mobile, and can punch through the heaviest armor.

Secondary Scorer: Canoptek Scarab Swarms
Dirt cheap and fast. While they can't hold objectives (OC 0), they are perfect for "screening" or exploding in the face of an enemy to slow them down.

Punchback: Canoptek Wraiths
Fast, move through walls, and possess a 4+ Invulnerable save. They are the premier counter-attackers used to intercept anything that threatens your backline.

Anvil: Lychguard
Armed with Hyperphase Swords and Dispersion Shields (4+ Invuln). When led by a Technomancer, they become nearly impossible to permanently kill.

Transport: Ghost Ark
More than a bus; it's a mobile repair station. It can trigger Reanimation Protocols for a nearby Warrior squad in your opponent's movement phase.

Orks

Leader: Ghazghkull Thraka
The Prophet of the Waaagh! He is a literal powerhouse that grants +1 to Hit and +1 to Wound for nearby infantry. He turns a simple charge into a mathematical massacre.

Objective Holder: Gretchin
At only 40 points, these are essential. Their "Thievin’ Scavengers" rule gives you a chance to generate an extra CP every turn they hold an objective.

Trade Unit: Squighog Boyz
Fast, aggressive, and "Tanky." They move 10" and hit like a truck on the charge. You throw them at an enemy flank to tie them up while the rest of the horde moves up.

Anti-Tank: Beast Snagga Boyz
While they are infantry, their "Monster Hunters" rule gives them +1 to Wound against Vehicles and Monsters, allowing a cheap squad of Orks to bring down a Knight.

Secondary Scorer: Stormboyz
Orks with rockets strapped to their backs. They can Deep Strike and move 12", making them perfect for zipping into corners to score Tactical Missions.

Punchback: Nobz
The "Elite" Orks. Armed with Power Klawz, they have a massive damage output. If an enemy touches your line, the Nobz charge in and delete them with S9 attacks.

Anvil: Meganobz
Clad in "Mega Armour," they have a 2+ Save. During the Waaagh!, they gain a 5+ Invulnerable and a 4+ Feel No Pain (if led by a Big Mek), making them an immovable brick.

Transport: Trukk
One of the best transports in the game. It’s cheap, has "Tank Shock" potential, and its "Careen" rule lets it move after exploding to cause more chaos.

T'au Empire

Leader: Commander Shadowsun
The supreme tactician. She provides a re-roll 1s to hit aura and is a "Lone Operative," meaning she can't be targeted from afar while she coordinates your fire base.

Objective Holder: Strike Team
While fragile, their "Suppression Fire" ability debuffs enemy hit rolls. They are best used to hold home objectives and act as Observers for your heavier guns.

Trade Unit: Breacher Team
High-risk, high-reward. Equipped with pulse blasters, they are designed to jump out of a Devilfish, vaporize an enemy off an objective, and likely die the next turn.

Anti-Tank: Broadside Battlesuits
The heavy hitters. Equipped with Heavy Rail Rifles, they provide high-strength, high-damage shots that can punch through the toughest armor from across the board.

Secondary Scorer: Stealth Battlesuits
Essential utility. They have Infiltrate and Forward Operative. They are also the best Observers in the army, granting the unit they guide re-rolls on 1s to wound.

Punchback: Farsight (Commander)
The rare T'au unit that wants to be close. He provides +1 to wound within 9" and is a capable melee fighter, acting as a "speed bump" for enemies charging your line.

Anvil: Ghostkeel Battlesuit
A massive "Lone Operative" suit. Because it can't be shot from more than 12" away and has "Stealth Drones" to negate damage, it is incredibly difficult for the enemy to remove.

Transport: Devilfish
Mandatory for Breachers. It provides the "Combat Debarkation" rule, allowing troops to move, disembark, and shoot with increased efficiency.

Tyranids

Leader: Hive Tyrant
The heart of the swarm. It allows you to use a Stratagem for 0CP even if you’ve already used it that phase, and its Synapse aura keeps your small bugs from fleeing.

Objective Holder: Termagants
Their "Slinking Horror" ability allows them to move D6" when an enemy gets close, making them slippery objective holders that are hard to pin down.

Trade Unit: Von Ryan’s Leapers
Fast, stealthy, and possess Fights First. They Infiltrate early to harass the enemy and die, buying time for your bigger monsters to move up.

Anti-Tank: Tyrannofex
Equipped with the Rupture Cannon, this is a dedicated "tank-deleter." Its "Resilient Organism" ability also reduces the Damage of an incoming attack to 0 once per game.

Secondary Scorer: Ripper Swarms
Tiny, cheap, and can be placed in Deep Strike. They are perfect for dropping into a table quarter to score Secondary Missions for almost zero point investment.

Punchback: Old One Eye
A melee powerhouse. He and his Carnifex bodyguards reroll all hits. If an enemy charges into them, he regenerates wounds and crushes them with monstrous claws.

Anvil: Maleceptor
High Toughness (T11) and a 4+ Invulnerable save. It has an aura that debuffs enemy Hit and Wound rolls, making everything around it harder to kill.

Transport: Tyrannocyte
A literal biological drop pod. It allows you to Deep Strike massive monsters (like the Tyrannofex or a Swarmlord) directly into the heart of the enemy lines.
