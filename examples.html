Felix' Warhammer List Building Guide

- Example Armies -

Here you will find the top three winning army lists aswell as some examples for each faction.

-Top 3-

1. Space Marines: Blade of Ultramar Winning List: Conan Jennings (AdeptiCon 2026 Champion, 9-0-0) The Ultramarines have pivoted away from the traditional "Calgar Castle" toward a high-mobility, high-pressure list that exploits the Gladius Task Force or the new Blade of Ultramar detachment. Why it's powerful: It has the best "Action Economy" in the game. With the ability to Advance and Charge or Fall Back and Shoot, they are never pinned down. Unit Roles: Leader: Marneus Calgar (Terminator Armor). He generates extra CP and provides incredible melee punch to a Terminator brick. Objective Holders: Heavy Intercessors. T6 and 3 wounds make them a nightmare to shift from the home objective. Trade Units: Assault Intercessors with Jump Packs. Fast, cheap, and capable of killing a unit twice their cost before dying. Anti-Tank: Gladiator Lancer. The most efficient long-range AT in the game due to its built-in rerolls. Secondary Scorers: Inceptors. Their 3" deep strike allows them to score "Behind Enemy Lines" or "Deploy Teleport Homers" anywhere on the board. Punchback: Brutalis Dreadnought. If an enemy charges your front line, this is the unit that crashes in next turn to wipe them out. Anvil: Terminator Squad (10-man). Combined with a Librarian for Sustained Hits, they sit in the center and simply refuse to die. Transport: Impulsor. Used to ferry small squads of Hellblasters or Company Heroes to mid-board safely.

2. Chaos Space Marines: Renegade Raiders Winning List: Brian Daugherty (AdeptiCon Runner-up, 8-1-0) This list focuses on the Renegade Raiders detachment, which grants extra AP and movement speed when targeting enemies on objectives. Why it's powerful: It is arguably the fastest army in the game. It uses "Scout" moves and "Dark Pacts" to ensure that whoever they hit stays dead. Unit Roles: Leader: Fabius Bile. He buffs a unit of Chosen to T5, making them significantly harder to kill in the mid-board. Objective Holders: Cultist Mob. Cheap, disposable, and they "Sticky" objectives so the rest of the army can move forward. Trade Units: Warp Talons. With their ability to go back into Strategic Reserves after killing a unit, they "trade" and then hide. Anti-Tank: Chaos Vindicator. The Demolisher Cannon is the ultimate "point and delete" tool for enemy armor. Secondary Scorers: Nurglings (Allied). Still the gold standard for cheap infiltrators that screen and score "Cleanse." Punchback: Chaos Chosen. They can Advance, Shoot, and Charge, making them the perfect reactionary unit. Anvil: Land Raider. Not just a transport, but a T12 brick that demands the opponent's entire shooting phase to kill. Transport: Rhino. Essential for protecting the Chosen and Legionaries as they race toward the center.

3. Necrons: Awakened Dynasty / Pantheon of Woe Winning List: Jordan Nach (Top 3 Major Placer) Despite point increases to C'tan, the Necrons remain dominant because their "stat checks" (High Toughness/Feel No Pain) are too much for many armies to handle. Why it's powerful: Reanimation Protocols. Even if you don't kill a unit entirely, it comes back. They play the "Primary Score" game better than anyone else. Unit Roles: Leader: Technomancer. Usually attached to Wraiths to give them a 5+ Feel No Pain, making them arguably the tankiest unit in the meta. Objective Holders: Immortals (with Plasmancer). They provide high-volume fire and are surprisingly durable for Battleline. Trade Units: Skorpekh Destroyers. They go in, kill an elite squad, and if they die, it’s fine—they’ve done their job. Anti-Tank: Canoptek Doomstalker. Since the 2026 update, these have become staple long-range fire support for the Awakened Dynasty. Secondary Scorers: Deathmarks. Snipers that can Deep Strike into corners to score "Investigate Signals." Punchback: C'tan Shard of the Nightbringer. The ultimate deterrent. If you move close to it, it will charge and destroy anything in the game. Anvil: Canoptek Wraiths. With 4 wounds, a 4++ Invuln, and a 5+++ FNP, they are the definition of an immovable object. Transport: Night Scythe / Monolith. While rarely used as a "bus," the Monolith’s ability to teleport units across the board serves as the ultimate tactical transport.
