Felix' Warhammer List Building Guide

- Roles -

Leader

The strategic backbone of your army. These are your Characters (Captains, Warbosses, Autarchs) that provide essential buffs, aura abilities, or CP (Command Point) generation. Primary Goal: To act as a force multiplier for a specific squad or to fulfill the "Warlord" requirement. Key Stat: Leadership (Ld) and "Leader" abilities that allow them to attach to bodyguard units.

Objective Holders

These units are the "sit and stay" specialists. They usually have high Objective Secured (OC) stats or enough durability to survive a round of enemy fire while camping on a circle. Primary Goal: Controlling Primary Objectives in your deployment zone or the mid-board. Key Stat: High OC (Objective Control) and cheap point costs.

Trade Units

Low-cost, high-damage units designed for "suicide missions." You send them in to destroy an enemy unit that costs significantly more than they do, fully expecting them to die in the next turn. Primary Goal: Winning the "points exchange" (e.g., a 100pt unit killing a 250pt unit). Key Stat: High Movement and high glass-cannon damage output.

Anti-Tank

Units equipped with high-Strength, high-Damage weaponry specifically designed to punch through the Toughness (T) of vehicles and monsters. Primary Goal: Removing heavy armor before it can trample your infantry. Key Stat: Weapons with Strength 12+ and $D6$ or flat 3-6 Damage.

Secondary Scorers

The utility players. These units are often fast, have the Infiltrators or Deep Strike keywords, and are kept in reserve to perform "Actions" or "Tactical Missions" like Behind Enemy Lines or Investigate Signals. Primary Goal: Scoring Victory Points (VP) rather than killing the enemy. Key Stat: High mobility and small unit footprints to fit into tight corners.

Punchback

These units hide behind your front lines or terrain. When an enemy charges your Objective Holders or Anvils, the Punchback unit moves in to retaliate and wipe the intruder off the board. Primary Goal: Punishing the enemy for committing to a charge. Key Stat: High Melee attacks and "Fights First" capabilities.

(Hammer and) Anvil

The ultimate defensive wall. These units are incredibly difficult to shift due to high Toughness, Invulnerable Saves, or Feel No Pain (FNP) rolls. They are designed to stand in the middle of the board and absorb the enemy's entire shooting phase. Primary Goal: To take a beating and refuse to move, creating a "roadblock" for the opponent. Key Stat: 2+ Armor Save, 4+ Invulnerable Save, and high Wounds.

Transport

Vehicles (Rhinos, Trukks, Falcons) used to ferry fragile or slow infantry across the board safely. Primary Goal: Protecting "Trade Units" or "Punchback" units from being shot before they reach their target. Key Stat: Movement speed and "Dedicated Transport" capacity.
